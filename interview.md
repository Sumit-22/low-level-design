# 🚀 Low-Level Design (LLD) — Complete Interview Guide

---

# 🔥 Standard LLD Interview Flow (Polished)

## 1. Requirement Clarification (5–10 min)
- Functional requirements  
- Non-functional requirements (scale, latency, consistency)  
- Constraints & assumptions  

---

## 2. High-Level Design (5 min)
- Components overview  
- Basic architecture  
- Data flow  

---

## 3. Entity Identification (5 min)
- Core objects  
- Relationships  

---

## 4. Class Design (10–15 min)
- Attributes  
- Methods  
- Interfaces  
- Apply SOLID principles  

---

## 5. Core Logic / Workflows (10 min)
- Business logic  
- State transitions  
- Algorithms  

---

## 6. Concurrency & Scalability (5–10 min)
- Thread safety  
- Locks / async processing  
- Race conditions  

---

## 7. Edge Cases (5 min)
- Failure handling  
- Invalid states  
- Limits & constraints  

---

## 8. Extensibility & Improvements (5–10 min)
- Future features  
- Design trade-offs  

---
