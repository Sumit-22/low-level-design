# 🚀 30-Day Startup Execution Plan – Smart Vending Platform

---
Alright — now we convert this into a real execution plan you could actually follow.
This is not theory — this is what you should do day-by-day to reach first revenue.

---
# 🎯 Goal (VERY CLEAR)

👉 In 30 days, you should have:

* 1 working smart vending machine
* Real users using it
* First ₹ earned

---

# 🧠 Strategy

👉 Don’t build a startup
👉 Build a **proof that money can be made**

---

# 📅 WEEK 1 – Problem Validation (MOST IMPORTANT)

---

## 🎯 Goal

👉 Confirm: **People will actually use & pay**

---

## ✅ Day 1–2: Market Research

Go physically:

* Colleges
* Gyms
* Office spaces

Ask:

* “Do you use vending machines?”
* “What problems do you face?”
* “Would you use UPI-based vending?”

---

## ✅ Day 3–4: Operator Research

Find local vending operators:

Ask:

* Revenue per machine
* Biggest pain
* Would they pay for analytics?

---

## ✅ Day 5–7: Decision

👉 If BOTH true:

* Users interested
* Operators interested

✅ Continue

❌ Else → pivot idea

---

# 📅 WEEK 2 – MVP BUILD (NO OVERENGINEERING)

---

## 🎯 Goal

👉 Build **basic working system**

---

## 🧩 MVP Features (ONLY THESE)

* QR payment (Razorpay / UPI)
* Inventory tracking
* Simple admin dashboard

---

## ⚙️ Tech Stack (FASTEST)

* Android app (Kotlin)
* Firebase (Auth + DB)
* Razorpay (payments)

---

## 🛠 Build Tasks

### Day 8–9

* Basic Android UI
* Scan QR → pay

---

### Day 10–11

* Backend (Firebase)
* Store transactions

---

### Day 12–14

* Simple dashboard
* Show:

  * sales
  * stock

---

👉 No Kafka, no microservices — **just working product**

---

# 📅 WEEK 3 – REAL WORLD TEST

---

## 🎯 Goal

👉 Put it in front of users

---

## ✅ Day 15–17

* Get 1 vending machine
  (borrow / partner / hack solution)

---

## ✅ Day 18–19

* Install QR + system

---

## ✅ Day 20–21

* Observe users

Track:

* Do they use it?
* Where do they struggle?

---
